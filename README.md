# Savora 🍽️✨ #

Savora is a beautifully crafted Android app designed to take the stress out of hosting high-end dining events. Instead of a standard ordering system, it invites guests to plan unforgettable private functions by easily exploring and customizing exclusive, per-person gourmet packages. Behind the scenes, Savora empowers restaurant staff with a robust management hub, giving them everything they need to seamlessly orchestrate reservations, edit menus, and deliver a flawless, upscale experience from the first tap to the final course.

---

## 🌟 Key Features 

### 👤 Customer Experience (Unauthenticated & Authenticated)
* *Premium Menu Showcase:* Anyone can open the app as a guest to browse visually striking menu package cards complete with high-resolution images, descriptive overviews, and base prices.
* *Intelligent Booking Flow:* Authenticated users access a dynamic booking form where they can select an event date, time, and guest count. 
* *Real-Time Cost Calculation:* Savora dynamically calculates costs on-the-fly (Base Price Per Guest × Number of Guests) and stacks additional service customization charges prominently before final confirmation.
* *Reservation Lifecycle Management:* From the "My Reservations" dashboard, users can track upcoming bookings, modify guest counts or menu packages (instantly triggering price recalculations), or cancel events via secure confirmation dialogs.

### 👑 Administrative Dashboard
* *Package CRUD Operations:* Managers can create, read, update, and delete menu configurations, dynamically altering pricing data, descriptions, and imagery across the platform.
* *User & Order Oversight:* Full access control to manage user accounts and globally monitor, edit, or cancel any customer reservation to align with real-world restaurant logistics.
